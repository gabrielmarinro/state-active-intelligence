# The Shift

Traditional AI systems often follow a simplified pattern:

**observe → infer → predict**

State-aware and closed-loop intelligence introduces a broader problem:

**observe → understand → predict → act → observe again → learn**

The distinction becomes important when actions can influence the state being modeled.

A recommendation can influence attention.

An intervention can influence behavior.

Behavior can change the state.

A changed state can change what becomes likely next.

The intelligence system therefore cannot be treated as completely external to the system it models.

The research question becomes:

> **What changes when intelligence is allowed to act within a system whose state evolves over time and can respond to that intelligence?**

This shift raises questions around:

- changing human state
- changing environments
- uncertainty
- human agency
- intervention effects
- consequences
- temporal learning

State-Active Intelligence explores this class of problems without assuming that the underlying system remains static.
