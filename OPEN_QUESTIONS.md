# Open Questions

The difficult part of evolving-system intelligence is not identifying that change exists.

It is determining **which changes matter, what they mean and what should happen next**.

## State

- When has a person or system changed enough that its previous representation is no longer reliable?
- How much historical behavior should remain relevant after a change?
- Which signals indicate that an earlier understanding is becoming less useful?

## Time

- How can intelligence distinguish a temporary event from a real transition?
- When should recent evidence outweigh a longer historical pattern?
- How should gradual change be recognized before it becomes obvious?

## Evidence

- How can observation remain distinct from interpretation?
- What should happen when two observations contradict each other?
- How should missing evidence be treated without confusing it with evidence of absence?

## Human behavior

- How should stated intention be compared with what a person actually does?
- How can intelligence reason about behavioral change without reducing a person to a label?
- How can temporary circumstances be separated from more persistent characteristics?

## Intervention

- What changes when an action by the intelligence can affect what happens next?
- How should outcomes be interpreted when both the environment and the intervention contributed to the change?
- How can intervention effects be evaluated without confusing correlation with causation?

## Decision quality

- What makes a decision useful when the future is uncertain?
- How should competing goals and constraints be represented?
- How can uncertainty be communicated without making the result unusable?

## Auditability

- What evidence should remain available to explain why a conclusion was reached?
- How can a changing system remain comparable across time?
- What does reproducibility mean when the underlying state itself changes?

## Governance

- Which boundaries should constrain action in human-centered intelligence?
- How should people challenge or override system conclusions?
- Which high-impact uses require additional safeguards before action?

## Architecture

- Which abstractions are fundamental to intelligence operating on evolving systems?
- Which responsibilities should remain separate to preserve epistemic integrity?
- Which architectural details can be discussed publicly without making a private system reconstructible?

These questions deliberately expose the problem space without publishing the implementation-specific answers.
