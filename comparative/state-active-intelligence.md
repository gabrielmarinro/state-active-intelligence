# State-Active Intelligence

State-Active Intelligence is a research label for intelligence operating on systems whose relevant conditions can change over time.

| Paradigm | Primary emphasis |
|---|---|
| Predictive AI | Estimating likely outcomes |
| Context-aware AI | Incorporating circumstances |
| Agentic AI | Planning and taking action |
| World models | Reasoning about possible system evolution |
| Decision intelligence | Improving consequential decisions |
| **State-Active Intelligence** | **Reasoning when the conditions being modeled can change and may be influenced by action** |

The distinction is conceptual.

It does not disclose the mechanisms, representations or architecture of any private system.
