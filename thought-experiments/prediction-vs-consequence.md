# Prediction vs Consequence

A prediction asks:

**What may happen if the current conditions continue?**

A consequence asks:

**What happened after an action or decision, and what changed because of it?**

In an evolving system, those questions can become connected because using a prediction can influence what happens next.

That creates an important distinction:

> **A forecast and the future it participates in are not the same analytical object.**

The public research examines that distinction without publishing the private mechanisms used to operationalize it.
