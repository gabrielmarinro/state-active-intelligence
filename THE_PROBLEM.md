# The Problem

AI systems increasingly understand context, maintain memory, use tools, reason over environments and execute actions.

A deeper problem emerges when the object being modeled is a changing human.

A human is a dynamic system rather than a static profile. Experience, beliefs, behavior, internal state, decisions, constraints and environment continuously interact.

The human also exists within a changing world. Economic conditions, social conditions, physical events, relationships, risks, opportunities and other external changes can alter how a person perceives a situation, evaluates alternatives and behaves.

Then the AI becomes part of that system.

A prediction can influence attention. An intervention can change behavior. Behavior changes the state. A changed state can change what becomes likely next.

The person may also evolve independently of any intervention. New experiences, new knowledge, repeated reflection, significant events and changing priorities can alter the personal model that previously explained the person's behavior.

Historical behavior therefore does not necessarily remain an accurate representation of current behavior.

The system being modeled is both dynamic and partially observable.

**The state being modeled becomes endogenous to the intelligence system.**

The question is no longer only:

> What is likely to happen?

It becomes:

> **What happens when intelligence models a changing human inside a changing environment, acts upon that system and continuously learns from the resulting state?**
