# Casebook

The public casebook is designed to make one phenomenon visible across several domains:

> The same domain intelligence can become less relevant when the relationship between the person, the domain and the surrounding context changes.

These are illustrative public fixtures.

They are intentionally mechanism-neutral.

## Healthcare

### H01 — Same Domain, Changing Engagement

**Initial state**

A person regularly interacts with a specialized health information service.

The domain knowledge remains stable.

The person's interaction pattern is initially consistent.

**Change**

Over time, the person's behavior changes.

Interaction becomes less frequent.

Previously accepted guidance receives weaker engagement.

The surrounding circumstances also change.

**Snapshot-oriented interpretation**

The person is still treated as broadly consistent with the earlier interaction pattern.

**Evolving-context interpretation**

The current relationship with the domain is treated as materially different from the earlier state.

**Observable phenomenon**

The domain did not change.

The person's relationship with the domain did.

---

### H02 — Intention vs Subsequent Behavior

**Initial state**

A person expresses an intention concerning a health-related process.

**Change**

Subsequent observed behavior diverges from the earlier intention.

**Snapshot-oriented interpretation**

The original intention remains heavily weighted.

**Evolving-context interpretation**

The divergence itself becomes relevant evidence about the current state.

**Observable phenomenon**

A stated intention is historical evidence.

Later behavior may provide different information about the current state.

## Legal

### L01 — Same Matter, Evolving Context

**Initial state**

A legal AI evaluates a matter using information available at an earlier stage.

**Change**

A later event materially changes the matter.

**Snapshot-oriented interpretation**

The earlier understanding remains the dominant reference.

**Evolving-context interpretation**

The current matter is re-evaluated using the changed context.

**Observable phenomenon**

The domain remains the same.

The matter does not.

---

### L02 — Evidence Changes Relevance

**Initial state**

Available documents support a working interpretation.

**Change**

A later document introduces information that qualifies the earlier interpretation.

**Snapshot-oriented interpretation**

The earlier conclusion remains comparatively stable.

**Evolving-context interpretation**

The new evidence changes the relevance and strength of the earlier interpretation.

**Observable phenomenon**

New evidence can change the meaning of historical evidence without changing the historical record itself.

## Finance

### F01 — Changing Personal Constraints

**Initial state**

A financial intelligence system evaluates a person using a set of known circumstances and stated preferences.

**Change**

Income, obligations, priorities or other relevant constraints change.

**Snapshot-oriented interpretation**

The earlier profile remains the primary basis of reasoning.

**Evolving-context interpretation**

The current relationship between the person, the financial domain and the changed constraints becomes the relevant object of reasoning.

**Observable phenomenon**

A stable domain does not imply a stable user context.

---

### F02 — Trust and Continued Use

**Initial state**

A person regularly relies on a financial AI service.

**Change**

Interaction patterns change after a sequence of recommendations or experiences.

**Snapshot-oriented interpretation**

The user remains modeled primarily from the previous interaction history.

**Evolving-context interpretation**

The recent interaction trajectory becomes evidence about the changing relationship with the system.

**Observable phenomenon**

Retention and trust can be viewed as evolving properties of the interaction rather than as fixed user attributes.

## Fleet / Operations

### O01 — Same System, Changing Operating Conditions

**Initial state**

An operational system is evaluated under a known set of conditions.

**Change**

Operating context changes materially.

**Snapshot-oriented interpretation**

The earlier assessment remains the main reference.

**Evolving-context interpretation**

Current conditions become the basis for reassessment.

**Observable phenomenon**

Historical operational regularity can become less representative after a meaningful state change.

---

### O02 — Action Changes What Comes Next

**Initial state**

An operational decision is made using the available state.

**Change**

The decision itself affects subsequent conditions.

**Snapshot-oriented interpretation**

Later observations are treated primarily as continuation of the earlier state.

**Evolving-context interpretation**

The later state is understood as occurring after a consequential action.

**Observable phenomenon**

The future environment can depend partly on what the intelligence or operator did earlier.

## Cross-domain observation

Across all domains, the central distinction remains:

**Knowing the domain is different from knowing how a particular person or system is currently interacting with that domain.**

The latter is a temporal problem.

It requires reasoning across change rather than reasoning from a frozen snapshot.
